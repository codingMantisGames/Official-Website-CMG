Wheel You Drive?

Grab a friend and take control of the same RC car — but each of you controls a different half. Player 1 handles front wheel steering, backward movement, and front wheel jump. Player 2 handles back wheel steering, forward movement, and back wheel jump. Communicate, coordinate, and make it through every obstacle together.
